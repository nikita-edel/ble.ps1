ble.ps1 is a line editor for PowerShell 7.4+ that behaves like
[ble.sh](https://github.com/akinomyoga/ble.sh). It takes over from PSReadLine when you type at
the prompt: highlighting, suggestions, completion, key bindings, history.

If you used ble.sh before, start with [Coming from ble.sh](Coming-from-ble.sh). The commands
(`bleopt`, `ble-face`, `ble-bind`, `ble-sabbrev`, `ble-import`) and most defaults are the same.
